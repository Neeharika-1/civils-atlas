## How can GST simplicity balance compliance, revenue and distribution?

**Study reference:** E6-2509-002

**Opening:** Council recommendations, operative notifications and product classifications are different legal layers.

**Dated evidence:** The main slabs are 5% and 18%. A special 40% demerit rate covers selected items.

**Core argument:** Simplification can reduce friction while affecting state revenue and credit chains.

**Counterpoint / evidence limit:** Do not say every product has only 5%/18%, or all tobacco rates changed on 22 September.

**Implementation lens:** Price pass-through depends on competition and input costs, not merely the statutory cut.
