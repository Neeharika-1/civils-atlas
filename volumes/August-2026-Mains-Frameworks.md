## Legislative productivity should be evaluated through deliberation as well as output.

**Study reference:** E15-2608-004

1. Anchor the reviewed 20 July–13 August session: 12 introduced, 11 passed, low scheduled-time use. Explain that these counts do not certify final commencement.
2. Distinguish introduction, committee referral, passage, assent and commencement; use the pending ISI Bill as an institutional-stage example.
3. Assess debate quality, committee evidence, fiscal scrutiny, stakeholder input and post-legislative review rather than a single passage total.
4. Balance timely lawmaking with adequate deliberation; propose better scheduling, published ministerial responses and scrutiny of delegated rules.
5. For APPSC, compare state legislative accountability without assuming identical procedures or inventing subsequent Assembly outcomes.
