#### 57th GST Council: distinguish recommendations from enacted powers
E17-2610-005 · P1 · 2026-10-08 · National / other

**Why in the news:** The 8 October Council release proposes process and enforcement reforms. Some later rules are reported by PRS, but the complete instrument-specific commencement chain was not inspected.

**Source-backed essentials**
- The Council recommends omitting CGST Section 69 arrest powers, raising prosecution threshold from ₹1 crore to ₹5 crore and reducing general penalty ceiling from ₹25,000 to ₹10,000.
- Small eligible e-commerce sellers could use an operator’s warehouse as principal place of business under proposed simplified registration conditions.
- Refund proposals include a ten-day acknowledgement/deficiency period and risk-based provisional processing. Different ITC reforms have proposed November 2026 or April 2027 timing; not all changes share one start date.

**Static connection · original guidance:** Article 279 A’s Council is an institutional forum for GST recommendations. Statutory amendment, delegated rules, portal capability and commencement are distinct steps.

- For AP taxpayers, distinguish the amount of tax, ITC passed on and aggregate turnover when explaining eligibility. A press release recommending repeal cannot itself establish that a statutory arrest power has already disappeared.
