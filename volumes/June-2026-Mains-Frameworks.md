# June 2026 · Eight original Mains frameworks

Original planning aids, not official answers or predictions. Use historical anchors and preserve all legal-stage and access cautions.

## Shared river governance needs cooperation without prejudging legal allocations.

**Study reference:** E13-2606-001

1. Anchor the June-indexed Tungabhadradiscussion and note the 31 July editorial posting; the 2019 Billrecommendation is not law.
2. Separate allocation adjudication, project objections, siltation and actual canal delivery. Connect Article 262 and Union/State Listentries.
3. Analyse asymmetry in upstream control, unreliable hydrological data and incentives to exaggerate drought distress.
4. For AP, propose shared gauges, transparent release accounts, tail-end measurement and ecological-flow safeguards with affected communities.
5. Conclude with a negotiation/escalation protocol that complements tribunals and judicial processes rather than asserting a committee supersedes them.

## India’s external resilience depends on the composition, not only the sign, of its current account.

**Study reference:** E13-2606-006

1. Anchor 8 June RBI release: Q4 surplus US$7.1 billion but full-year deficit US$25.2 billion; both preliminary.
2. Decompose goods−83.4, services+60.4, primaryincome−11.1 and secondary+41.3 billion; allow rounding, not an invented residual.
3. Explain gross personal transfers 43.5 billion versus net secondary income; FDI/FPI are financial flows, not current-account remittances.
4. Consider services concentration, overseas employment cycles, energy prices and foreign-exchange valuation risks. For AP, consider migrant households without claiming a new state estimate.
5. Recommend export diversification, skills, lower remittance costs and prudent external financing; a one-quarter surplus is not immunity to shocks.

## Temporary fraud compensation should be clear about eligibility, incentives and the sunset.

**Study reference:** E13-2606-011

1. Anchor 24 June directions applying to specified transactions from 1 January 2027; distinguish liability rules from limited compensation.
2. Use gross≤₹50,000 eligibility, min(85% net loss, ₹25,000), timely reporting to bank+1930/portal, lifetime claim and one-yearloss window.
3. Illustrate ₹40,000 loss minus ₹15,000 recovery=₹25,000 net loss and ₹21,250 eligible compensation; later recoveries require recalculation.
4. Analyse inclusion and trust benefits alongside moral hazard, proof burdens, exclusion by reporting delays and administrative cost.
5. Recommend victim assistance, consistent bank reasoning, accessible appeals and independent monitoring; do not weaken prevention or promise every victim full repayment.

## A per-person food formula can retain household-size inequity when capped.

**Study reference:** E13-2606-016

1. Anchor DFPD consultation, draft 7 kg/person, max 35 kg/AAY household; existing reviewed law 35 kg/household and PHH 5 kg/person.
2. Illustrate three persons 21 kg, five 35 kg, sevenstill 35 kg. Smaller AAY households can lose versus the existing 35 kg.
3. Examine adequacy, diet diversity, household identification and the cap’s effect on large vulnerable families.
4. For AP, seek household-size distribution, appeal safeguards and transparent analysis before changing allocations; do not call consultation an enacted cut.
5. Conclude that a reform’s stated rationalisation objective must be tested against the exact formula, transition protection and nutrition outcomes.

**Conclusion test: ** Name a responsible institution, an implementation safeguard and a measurable outcome.

## An ambulance system is a clinical referral network, not merely a vehicle fleet.

**Study reference:** E13-2606-019

1. Anchor 29 June NAS guidelines, AIS 125, integrated dispatch, GPS/GISand progressive 112 integration.

3. For AP, consider tribal/hill access, highway trauma, flood constraints, EMTs kills and hospital bed/critical-care readiness.
4. Analyse maintenance, contract incentives, privacy and the risk of optimising average response time while remote patients remain underserved.
5. Recommend equity-sensitive performance measures, clinical audits, grievance access and interoperable dispatch; do not report guidelines as universal compliance.
