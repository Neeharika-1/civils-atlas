## Answer key and explanations

**MAY-01. A** — The release reported annual real GDP growth of 6.5% and Q4 growth of 7.4%, using that release vintage. Study reference: MY25-001.

**MAY-02. A** — Negotiation conclusion, signature and entry into force are different milestones. Social-security contribution arrangements are not general tax exemptions. Study reference: MY25-002.

**MAY-03. A** — Terms of reference set scope; they do not establish that a CEPA has entered into force. Study reference: MY25-003.

**MAY-04. A** — Its objective is remission of embedded duties and taxes, subject to eligibility and rates. May restoration for categories took effect from June. Study reference: MY25-004.

**MAY-05. B** — Guarantees support eligible lending and do not remove borrower repayment duties or underwriting requirements. Study reference: MY25-005.

**MAY-06. A** — Announced support prices do not themselves prove universal procurement or a legal buying obligation for every unit. Study reference: MY25-006.

**MAY-07. A** — The dated release retains the structure; the February Budget enhancement announcement must not be silently substituted for May operational terms. Study reference: MY25-007.

**MAY-08. B** — A draft is not enacted law, and document registration is not universally a conclusive title guarantee. Study reference: MY25-008.

**MAY-09. C** — Classification can guide finance but is not itself loan approval or credit issuance. Study reference: MY25-009.

**MAY-10. A** — The RBI Governor chairs it; the board should not be confused with the Monetary Policy Committee. Study reference: MY25-010.

**MAY-11. C** — Investor-level sublimits and aggregate/regulatory conditions are distinct. Avoid treating a targeted relaxation as total deregulation. Study reference: MY25-011.

**MAY-12. C** — A fuel linkage differs from a PPA and does not guarantee plant profitability or remove other regulatory duties. Study reference: MY25-012.

**MAY-13. A** — The historical May decision protected previously granted ECs and was followed by further litigation. Read current binding orders before relying on the rule today. Study reference: MY25-014.

# May 2025 · Original Mains answer laboratory

These are practice frameworks, not model answers or examination predictions. Use the historical May evidence and check later legal/implementation changes. Broad AP links do not constitute exact APPSC paper mapping.

## 1. From trade agreement to realised exports · 250 words
Prompt: Negotiated market access is necessary but insufficient for export competitiveness. Discuss using the India–UK announcement.
Opening: Distinguish the May conclusion of negotiations from signature and entry into force.
Mechanism: Lower tariffs and services opportunities → compliance with origin/standards → competitive production → actual sales.
Balance: Product exclusions, adjustment costs, utilisation by MSMEs and non-tariff barriers can limit benefits. The social-security arrangement is distinct from an income-tax exemption.
AP application: Use product-specific opportunities for state exporters only after checking schedules and implementation dates; do not invent an export gain.
Conclusion: Pair diplomacy with logistics, standards, skills and accessible compliance support.
Study references: MY25-002, MY25-003, MY25-004.

## 2. Climate finance without greenwashing · 250 words
Prompt: Transition finance must recognise development constraints without legitimising indefinite high emissions.
Opening: A taxonomy classifies economic activities; it does not itself provide funding or generate carbon credits.
Mechanism: Clear criteria → credible disclosure → comparable investment choices → monitored transition outcomes.
Balance: Use measurable pathways, transparent boundaries and review dates; test additionality and carbon-lock-in risks.
Application: Explain why renewable attribute ownership under a proposed VPPA must be distinguished from physical electricity supply. Add the April hydrogen production-boundary example if useful.
Conclusion: Credible verification and review are more important than a green label.
Study references: MY25-009, MY25-016; April magazine hydrogen supplement.

## 3. Reliable public infrastructure in Andhra Pradesh · 250 words
Prompt: Announced investment is not equivalent to delivered public value. Discuss with Amaravati and Quantum Valley.
Opening: Identify the May infrastructure relaunch and the envisioned quantum installation as different types of development initiatives.
Mechanism: Finance and agreements → permissions/procurement → construction → operations → measurable benefits.
Balance: Land-pooling commitments, flood resilience, ongoing operating costs and transparent milestones matter. The IBM announcement includes export-licence/definitive-agreement conditions.
Evaluation: For a capital city use service accessibility, resilience and sustainable finance. For quantum investment use skills, research access and useful validated applications—not qubit count alone.
Conclusion: Publish stage-specific progress and outcome evidence instead of adding targets to achievements.
Study references: MY25-019, MY25-020, MY25-021.

## 4. Agricultural support beyond headline prices · 150 words
Prompt: Higher support prices and cheaper credit cannot by themselves ensure resilient farm incomes.
Opening: Use common paddy MSP ₹2,369 per quintal for the announced 2025–26 season, clearly dated.
Mechanism: Price support and seasonal credit help when procurement, quality requirements, lending access and timely payment work.
Balance: Water stress, input costs, climate shocks and market concentration remain. Do not confuse the Budget KCC enhancement ambition with the explicit May MISS continuation terms.
AP application: Assess actual procurement and fisheries-credit conditions without assuming universal eligibility.
Conclusion: Combine risk management, extension, diversified markets and reliable implementation.
Study references: MY25-006, MY25-007.

## 5. Labour statistics and human development · 250 words
Prompt: Explain why headline unemployment and a national HDI rank are incomplete measures of development.
Opening: The first monthly PLFS bulletin published in May refers to April; HDR 2025’s India HDI value refers to 2023.
Mechanism: Employment participation, work availability, earnings, health and education capture different capabilities.
Balance: Compare like age groups and reference periods; UR uses the labour force, while WPR/LFPR use population. National means conceal group and regional disparities.
Policy: Link ITI upgradation to completion, placement quality and wages; link welfare to disaggregated health/learning outcomes.
Conclusion: A dashboard with well-defined denominators is more useful than competing isolated rankings.
Study references: MY25-015, MY25-027, MY25-034, MY25-035.

## 6. Technology claims and ethical evidence · 150 words
Prompt: How should public policy respond to promising but early genome-editing applications?
Opening: Contrast announced edited rice varieties with a personalised somatic therapy for an infant.
Mechanism: Precise intervention → delivery and biological effect → validation → monitoring → responsible adoption.
Balance: Off-target effects, environment/clinical context, consent, cost and access require context-specific safeguards. One case is not a universal cure, and trial performance is not a farm-level guarantee.
AP application: Connect rice relevance with local agronomy and seed systems without claiming universal availability.
Conclusion: Encourage innovation through transparent evidence and proportionate, adaptive oversight.
Study references: MY25-024, MY25-037.

## 7. Deterrence, escalation and information integrity · 250 words
Prompt: Effective counterterrorism requires disciplined military and public communication. Discuss.
Opening: Date and attribute the 7 May Operation Sindoor statement rather than repeating every battlefield claim as independently established.
Mechanism: Defined objectives, credible capability, integrated defence and clear signalling interact with diplomacy and escalation control.
Balance: Civilian protection, uncertain information and adversarial misinformation require verification; a system inventory is not proof of a specific engagement.
Institutions: Use appropriate command processes and reliable dated briefings. Do not conflate rhetorical descriptions with a formal change to nuclear doctrine.
Conclusion: Credibility comes from capability and evidentiary discipline, not the volume of unsupported claims.
Study references: MY25-030, MY25-031, MY25-038.

## 8. Emergency care as a delivery system · 150 words
Prompt: A cashless-care entitlement is necessary but insufficient for effective road-trauma response.
Opening: State the notified cap of ₹1.5 lakh for seven days at designated hospitals, not an unlimited universal benefit.
Mechanism: Incident detection → safe transport → timely triage/admission → treatment → claims → rehabilitation.
Balance: Rural access, hospital refusal, fund settlement and follow-up affect real outcomes. Prevention, enforcement and road design remain essential.
AP application: Track time-to-treatment and referral failures across districts rather than only expenditure or enrolment.
Conclusion: Connect finance with accountable trauma networks and prevention.
Study references: MY25-018.
