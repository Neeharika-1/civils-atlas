### 07 · Digital fraud — liability allocation

E13-2606-010 · P1 · 2026-06-24 · National / other

**Why in the news:** The 24 June directions apply to electronic transactions undertaken on or after 1 January 2027.

**Source-backed essentials**

### 08 · Small-fraud compensation — eligibility and arithmetic

#### Fraud compensation: gross threshold, net-loss calculation and sunset
E13-2606-011 · P1 · 2026-06-24 · National / other

**Why in the news:** The same 24 June directions create a limited compensation mechanism for specified bona fide victims.

**Source-backed essentials**
- Eligible complaints involve gross loss up to ₹50,000; compensation is the lower of 85% of net loss and ₹25,000.
- The victim must report to both the bank and 1930/cybercrime portal within five calendar days; eligibility includes a bona fide determination.
- The benefit is once in a lifetime; the mechanism covers qualifying losses occurring up to one year from the directions’ effective date.

**Static connection · original guidance:** Gross loss determines the threshold, while net loss deducts recoveries. This mechanism addresses specified customer-negligence cases, not a universal replacement for zero-liability rules.
