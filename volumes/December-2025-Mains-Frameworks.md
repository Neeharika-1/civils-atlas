# December 2025 · Eight original Mains frameworks

Original planning aids, not official answers or predicted questions. Evidence is dated; recommendations below are analytical guidance.

## How can nuclear expansion reconcile investment, safety and compensation?

**Study reference:** E7-2512-005

**Opening distinction:** Legislative passage, assent, commencement, licensing and plant commissioning are distinct milestones.

**Dated anchor:** It provides for licences to specified Indian private entities. Operator-liability tiers range from ₹100 crore to ₹3,000 crore by capacity.

**Argument:** Investment mobilisation needs independent safety capacity and transparent compensation arrangements.

**Counterargument / limit:** Do not describe unrestricted foreign operation or new private plants already commissioned from passage alone.

**Implementation priorities:** Independent safety capability, licensing capacity and transparent compensation.

**Evaluation:** Operational safety, emergency readiness and timely victim compensation.

**AP / linkage angle:** This note records the reviewed passage-stage framework, not verification of every later operative rule.

**Suggested 150–250-word structure:** Define the mechanism in two sentences; cite the dated anchor; develop two benefits and two constraints; propose the priorities above with a responsible institution; conclude using the evaluation criterion rather than another announcement.

## How should an employment guarantee combine entitlement, fiscal sharing and timely delivery?

**Study reference:** E7-2512-011

**Opening distinction:** A household guarantee is not an individual guaranteed salary; law, budgets, work demand and actual payments differ.

**Dated anchor:** The guarantee increases from 100 to 125 days per rural household. The reviewed ordinary-state funding ratio is 60:40; specified special states use 90:10.

**Argument:** Compare expanded days with state fiscal exposure and enforceability.

**Counterargument / limit:** Do not say 125 days were automatically worked/paid by every rural person immediately upon passage.

**Implementation priorities:** Demand capture, fiscal sharing, wage payments and state contingency planning.

**Evaluation:** Timely work/payment and enforceable unemployment allowance.

**AP / linkage angle:** AP analysis should assess wage-payment capacity and demand responsiveness, not only the additional 25 days.

**Suggested 150–250-word structure:** Define the mechanism in two sentences; cite the dated anchor; develop two benefits and two constraints; propose the priorities above with a responsible institution; conclude using the evaluation criterion rather than another announcement.

## How can insurance liberalisation deepen protection rather than ownership change alone?

## How should public skilling programmes be judged beyond certificates?

**Study reference:** E7-2512-025

**Opening distinction:** The denominator excludes some components; a placement ratio does not necessarily establish sustained employment or adequate pay.

**Dated anchor:** The review reports 23 lakh placements out of 56 lakh certified in specified short-term/special-project components. Skill-gap planning and convergence were weak.

**Argument:** Align training with district demand and verify retention, wages and employer feedback.

**Counterargument / limit:** The reported 41% is component- and audit-period-specific, not an all-time unemployment rate.

**Implementation priorities:** District skill-demand studies, credible assessment and employer feedback.

**Evaluation:** Retention and earnings after placement, independently sampled.

**AP / linkage angle:** Do not label this a current placement rate for every phase or every trained person.

## How should groundwater policy integrate quantity, quality and local demand?

**Study reference:** E7-2512-030

**Opening distinction:** Stage of extraction uses annual extraction divided by annual extractable resources, not total recharge.

**Dated anchor:** Annual recharge is reported as 448.52 BCM. Extractable resources are 407.75 BCM and extraction 247.22 BCM.

**Argument:** A national average below 100% does not establish every block is safe or water is uncontaminated.

**Evaluation:** Seasonal levels and quality alongside transparent extractable-resource estimates.
