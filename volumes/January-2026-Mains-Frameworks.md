# January 2026 · Eight original Mains frameworks

Original planning aids, not official answers or exam predictions. Historical source claims and proposals need current-status checks.

## How should fiscal consolidation protect valuable public services and investment?

**Study reference:** E8-2601-005

**Opening distinction:** Revenue expenditure classification does not alone determine social value; effective capex differs from only direct capital outlay.

**Dated anchor:** It expects the Centre’s FY2025–26 fiscal deficit below 4.5% of GDP. Effective capital expenditure includes grants for creation of capital assets.

**Main argument:** Evaluate outcomes and financing sustainability rather than call all transfers wasteful or all capex productive.

**Counterpoint / evidence limit:** A Survey expectation is not an audited final deficit or proof every state has the same fiscal position.

**Implementation priorities:** Trace recurrent commitments, guarantees and capital-grant outcomes, rather than use accounting labels alone.

**Evaluation criterion:** Service outcomes and medium-term debt/fiscal flexibility.

**Linkage / AP application:** AP fiscal analysis needs state-specific accounts, guarantees and payment obligations.

**Suggested answer plan:** Define the mechanism, cite the dated anchor, develop two benefits and two constraints, name accountable implementing institutions, and conclude with the evaluation criterion. Keep court stays, consultations and commencement stages explicit.

## What determines whether negotiated market access becomes usable export opportunity?

**Study reference:** E8-2601-006

**Opening distinction:** Tariff-line counts, trade-value coverage, negotiation conclusion and legal entry into force differ.

**Dated anchor:** The primary announcement describes preferential access for over 99% of Indian exports by trade value. Tariff reductions include entry-into-force and schedule conditions.

**Main argument:** AP marine, textile and food exporters also need origin, sanitary and traceability compliance.

**Counterpoint / evidence limit:** Do not call every product instantly duty-free or replace trade-value coverage with tariff-line share.

**Implementation priorities:** Combine tariff-origin guidance, sanitary compliance, emissions records and finance for smaller firms.

**Evaluation criterion:** Preference utilisation and durable market diversification, not treaty headlines.

**Linkage / AP application:** Read EFTA, UK, Oman and NZ notes as separate agreements with different stages.

**Suggested answer plan:** Define the mechanism, cite the dated anchor, develop two benefits and two constraints, name accountable implementing institutions, and conclude with the evaluation criterion. Keep court stays, consultations and commencement stages explicit.

## How can equity safeguards be both effective and procedurally fair?

**Study reference:** E8-2601-011

**Opening distinction:** An interim stay is not final invalidation; a notified framework may be temporarily inoperative.

**Dated anchor:** The regulations envisage Equal Opportunity Centres and Equity Committees. The Court identified ambiguities and potential misuse concerns.

**Main argument:** Institutional inclusion needs clear definitions and fair accessible procedures.

**Counterpoint / evidence limit:** Do not describe the 2026 framework as unconditionally in force or the stay as abolishing all anti-discrimination duties.

**Implementation priorities:** Clear definitions, accessible complaints and fair reasoned decisions alongside compliance with court orders.

**Evaluation criterion:** Inclusion and trusted redress without assuming a stayed rule is operative.

**Linkage / AP application:** Track later court orders rather than present every proposed 2026 deadline as operative.

**Suggested answer plan:** Define the mechanism, cite the dated anchor, develop two benefits and two constraints, name accountable implementing institutions, and conclude with the evaluation criterion. Keep court stays, consultations and commencement stages explicit.

## What makes student wellbeing a functioning institutional duty rather than a helpline poster?

**Study reference:** E8-2601-012

**Opening distinction:** Screening/support is not a substitute for clinical diagnosis and appropriate referral.

**Dated anchor:** It envisages support centres and monitoring committees. Helpline links with Tele-MANAS and district/national programmes feature.

**Main argument:** Confidentiality, trained staffing and continuity determine access beyond announcing a centre.

**Counterpoint / evidence limit:** Guidance is not proof every campus has staffed services or a guarantee crises cannot occur.

**Linkage / AP application:** Do not treat all sensitive student data as routinely publishable performance material.

## How can waste regulation turn formal duties into reliable local service?

**Study reference:** E8-2601-018

**Opening distinction:** Notification and commencement differ; a rulebook does not create collection/processing capacity by itself.

**Dated anchor:** The stated effective date is 1 April 2026. Applicability extends to rural local bodies.

## How can urban water governance prevent rather than only respond to contamination?

**Study reference:** E8-2601-030

**Opening distinction:** Supply infrastructure, continuous pressure, safe quality and accessible complaint handling are different service dimensions.

**Dated anchor:** The article reports NHRC scrutiny of municipal supply vulnerability. Water quality, sanitation and network integrity interact.

**Main argument:** AP towns need surveillance, testing, pipe/sewer mapping and rapid alerts.

**Counterpoint / evidence limit:** Connections alone do not certify potable water or every reported pathogen in every household sample.

**Implementation priorities:** Network mapping, independent quality sampling, pressure integrity and rapid public warnings.

**Evaluation criterion:** Safe reliable delivered water, not only connections.

**Linkage / AP application:** Do not infer a final lab-confirmed causal finding for every illness from one secondary account.

**Suggested answer plan:** Define the mechanism, cite the dated anchor, develop two benefits and two constraints, name accountable implementing institutions, and conclude with the evaluation criterion. Keep court stays, consultations and commencement stages explicit.

## How can AP mobilise infrastructure capital while controlling contingent fiscal risk?

**Study reference:** E8-2601-039

**Opening distinction:** A holding company, pooled investment fund and project SPV serve different functions and carry different risks.

**Dated anchor:** APLINC is described as an integrated infrastructure holding company. The AP Logistics Fund is proposed as an AIF.

**Main argument:** Institutional mobilisation needs governance, investor protections and credible project cash flows.

**Counterpoint / evidence limit:** Do not call an AIF a universal government grant or announced sponsor share a completed contribution.

**Implementation priorities:** Separate sponsor, fund manager and project roles with transparent procurement and risk disclosure.

**Evaluation criterion:** Funds actually raised, projects delivered and contingent exposure reported.

**Linkage / AP application:** Cabinet approval does not prove SEBI registration, full fundraising or asset construction.

**Suggested answer plan:** Define the mechanism, cite the dated anchor, develop two benefits and two constraints, name accountable implementing institutions, and conclude with the evaluation criterion. Keep court stays, consultations and commencement stages explicit.
