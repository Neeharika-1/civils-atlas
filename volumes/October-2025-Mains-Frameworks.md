## How can distribution reform reconcile viability and universal access?

**Study reference:** E6-2510-008

**Opening:** A subsidy promise, budgeted transfer and utility receipt are different financial stages.

**Dated evidence:** Distribution reform features in the draft. Tariffs, cost recovery and subsidy arrangements require separate scrutiny.

**Core argument:** Efficiency and contestability need universal-service and consumer safeguards.

**Counterpoint / evidence limit:** Do not claim draft provisions already changed every consumer tariff.

**Implementation lens:** AP analysis should examine farm support, utility finances and actual state implementation.
