## Discuss fiscal consolidation and expenditure quality using the Union Budget 2026–27.

**Study reference:** CA-002

**Dated anchor: ** The Union Budget was presented on 1 February.

1. Define BE/RE/actuals and the fiscal versus revenue deficit. Use the 4.3% fiscal target as a dated Budget-day estimate.
2. Argue that productive investment and effective services can reinforce medium-term revenue capacity.
3. Counterpoint: unspent allocations, optimistic receipts and weak project preparation can undermine both growth and consolidation.
4. Recommend realistic revenue scenarios, procurement readiness, milestone disclosure and independent outcome evaluation.
5. Do not recompute Budget-day ratios with the later GDP vintage without clearly labelling a new calculation.

**Evidence caution: ** The target is not the realised deficit, and expenditure growth is not automatically inflation-adjusted.

## Can market-linked urban challenge funding improve municipal services?

**Study reference:** E9-2602-023

**Dated anchor: ** The Cabinet approved central assistance of ₹1 lakhcrore.

1. Use the 25% central/at-least 50% market-financing design as an anchor, not an unconditional city entitlement.
