The editorial contract

01 / Source ladder

Gazette / judgment / official data → PIB and ministries → PRS and original reports → newspapers for scrutiny and context → exam compilations for gap discovery only.

02 / Note structure

Subject and block · dated trigger · facts · static connection · analysis · common trap · Mains prompt · sources · access caveat. Mains prompts are original study guidance, not sourced predictions.

03 / Priority order

P1: core constitutional, economic, environmental, scientific and AP developments. P2: supporting governance and sectoral detail. P3: selective appointments, awards, sports and miscellaneous recall; wider backlog remains.

04 / Revision loop

Read P1 first. Recall after 1, 7 and 30 days. For Prelims, explain the trap unaided. For Mains, write a 150-word answer with a dated fact and a balanced conclusion.

Full-coverage review protocol

  1. Inventory every day and ministry, then reconcile monthly PIB and PRS releases.
  2. Cross-check two newspaper streams, including an Andhra Pradesh section.
  3. Add AP GOs, budget documents, socio-economic surveys and district-specific developments.
  4. Reconcile reports, international bodies, courts, RBI, Parliament and scientific agencies.
  5. Track excluded ceremonial/duplicate items with a reason; no month is complete until exceptions and failures are resolved.
  6. Separate proposal, approval, notification, commencement, implementation and later amendment. Re-check volatile statistics before exams.
