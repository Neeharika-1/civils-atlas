## Important distinctions retained

- CBRO 35%/55%/75% are differently scoped cumulative ceilings, not additive quarterly shares. Listed exceptions and BRO conditions matter.
- A government's allocation memo, internal territorial table or outreach instruction is not actual expenditure or proof every public meeting occurred. The 11 March memo's 28-district wording is not independent evidence of district-reorganisation law.
- Government-department prepaid billing is not a universal household-meter order. DDO mapping differs from HOD bill generation and treasury audit.
- ASI FY2024–25 reference period, October 2025–June 2026 fieldwork and November publication are separate. Capital-size bands do not define current statutory MSME categories; value growth is not silently labelled real growth.
- Horizon Europe negotiation conclusion differs from signature, operative association and a competitive grant. January 2027 is expected reporting, not verified completed entry into force. EU background distinguishes the 15 July TTC and 17 July start of formal talks.
- NSQ and authorised-batch-release announcements are draft proposals. The session-bound Gazette request returned an application runtime-error page; it is not a legal instrument. The 30-day consultation trigger is public availability of Gazette copies, not automatically PIB's date.
- PM-JAY, ABHA, ABDM, NHCX and AI validation are different functions. NHA demonstrations are not independent tests; the demonstrated voice scribe requires doctor review.
- Watershed draft consultation is not final NTG adoption or WDC-PMKSY 3.0 sanction; Odisha/Karnataka project references do not establish AP inclusion.
- Grievance enquiry: seven working days; resolution: 15 days from receipt. Do not sum them sequentially or silently relabel both as working days. Original appeal schedule unread.
- SARAS administrative turnover/footfalls are not audited profit, net household income or unique beneficiaries. PIB's inauguration chronology and TOI's 11-day description are not silently reconciled; no definitive duration is inferred.
- MLFF design is not toll waiver, a GNSS-only mandate or completed national deployment. TRIFED market facilitation is conditional, not assured procurement.
